About AccurateScribe.ai – Transcribe

AccurateScribe is an AI-powered speech-to-text and transcription app built for modern workflows. Powered by advanced Whisper technology, it delivers fast, accurate Whisper transcription and securely converts audio and video into readable, editable text. Transcribe meetings, podcasts, interviews, lectures, and other recordings—including recorded Zoom meetings—in minutes. Easily create meeting transcripts and automatic notes, repurpose podcast transcripts into new content, and generate subtitles and captions. Key features: Whisper-powered AI transcription for audio and video files Fast speech-to-text processing Up to 99.8% accuracy for clear audio Meeting transcription and automatic notes Podcast transcription and content repurposing Subtitle and caption generation Searchable and shareable transcripts Easy editing, translation, and export Support for more than 134 transcription languages Automatic speaker identification and timestamps Translate transcripts and subtitles into nearly 250 languages Large-file and batch processing Export to DOCX, PDF, TXT, SRT, and VTT Secure, encrypted file handling Ideal for remote teams, businesses, content creators, journalists, students, researchers, and professionals who need reliable Whisper transcription for meetings, documentation, collaboration, subtitle creation, and content repurposing.
