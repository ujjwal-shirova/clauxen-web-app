About Monoblend AI Barista

Monoblend AI Barista matches a user's described taste to the six-coffee Monoblend specialty lineup and then supports the practical steps that follow. From a description such as chocolatey and low-acid, bright and fruity, a gift, or decaf for the evening, it returns a Safe, a Curious and a Wild pick with a match score and the reason each one fits, and refines those picks from plain feedback like 'too sour' or 'I want it fuller'. Beyond matching, it generates brewing recipes tuned per method and per bean, pairs coffee with a described dish or a photo of a plate, diagnoses a cup that came out bitter, sour or watery, compares two beans side by side, builds a guided tasting flight for guests and gifts, and explains acidity, body, roast and processing in plain language. The app is informational only: it does not sell coffee, display prices, take orders or report stock, and it declines rather than inventing commercial or store details it does not hold.
