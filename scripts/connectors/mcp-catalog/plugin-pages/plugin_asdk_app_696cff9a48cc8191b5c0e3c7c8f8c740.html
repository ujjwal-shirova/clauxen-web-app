About Pet Project Co-Founder AI

"Pet Project Co-Founder AI" is your market research assistant. It will help you validate product ideas using real data from trusted market research services. No more guesses or hallucinations. Instead of relying on plain LLM-only answers, you will use raw market data interpreted by ChatGPT as clear insights. You do not need prior marketing experience. You just ask a question and the app builds a market research plan for you. The plan will include gathering data from third-party services. You are guided through what services to use and what queries to run there. After you use the recommended service, you copy-paste their data back to the app and get a clear interpretation from ChatGPT. Examples of questions you can get answers to: - "I have an idea for the app X. Is there enough demand to justify building it?" - "What are people searching for when they want Y?" - "Who exactly is my target user for the app Z? What platforms do they use?" - "I built an app W. Where do I find users for it?" For now, the following services are supported: - SparkToro — audience intelligence (who your users are, what they read, who they follow) - Ubersuggest — keyword demand and SEO metrics - AnswerThePublic — real search questions and phrasing Disclaimers: - This app does not automatically gather data from the recommended market research services. The app helps with building search queries for these services and reporting the resulting data back. But you will need to visit and use these services yourself. We do not automatically scrape data from these services. - This app does not provide paid subscriptions to the recommended market research services. You use these services according to their terms of use. - This app currently supports a limited set of services: SparkToro, Ubersuggest, and AnswerThePublic. Only these services may be recommended during marketing research. Keep in mind that there are other good services for market research on the internet, we just do not support them yet. You can request adding new services by emailing the [developer@petprojectcofounder.ai](mailto:developer@petprojectcofounder.ai) - For the best experience, it is recommended to use this app on desktop
