About RoTV Guide

RoTV Guide answers “what should I watch tonight?” for Romania with real, continuously refreshed data ChatGPT does not have: live EPG for 258 Romanian TV channels, current streaming catalogues (Netflix, HBO Max, Disney+, Prime Video, including the Netflix Top 10 for Romania), cinema schedules, box office and theatre programs. Recommendations are deterministic and explainable — by mood, for couples, full-evening planning — and every answer can disclose how fresh its data is. No account, no ads, no tracking; all listings come from official broadcaster and platform schedules with per-answer freshness metadata.
