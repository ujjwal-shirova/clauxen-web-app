About Revise

Revise connects ChatGPT and other AI agents to a fully featured word processor. Create, import, search, and edit real documents; collaborate across hundreds of pages; review AI edits as tracked changes; manage layouts, footnotes, and collaborators; and export to DOCX, PDF, Markdown, HTML, or plain text.
