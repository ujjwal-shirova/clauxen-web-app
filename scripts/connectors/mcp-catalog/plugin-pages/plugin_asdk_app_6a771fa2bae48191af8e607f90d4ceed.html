About Dropshipping Product Scout

Dropshipping Product Scout is an independent, read-only research tool that uses CJ Dropshipping API data to help users review a small, manually curated, general-audience catalog. Search by keyword or an approved CJ product code; inspect sale status, approved variants, public prices, and normalized warehouse inventory; then estimate shipping methods, transit windows, and USD postage for one approved variant. It does not access CJ accounts, customer orders, addresses, tracking, or payment data, and it cannot reserve stock, place orders, book shipments, publish listings, or make purchases. Availability and shipping are current estimates from the data source and can change. CJ Dropshipping and OpenAI do not sponsor or endorse this independent app.
