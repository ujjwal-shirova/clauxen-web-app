About Ordering.Tools

Search for restaurants powered by Ordering.Tools, read their full menus — with prices, allergens, dietary options, and modifiers — and prepare orders ready for checkout. Ask "what gluten-free starters do you have at Yugo Bistro?" or "order me a margherita and a Coke from London Pub for pickup, my name is John, phone +359..." and the assistant builds the cart, validates pricing against the venue's live menu, and hands you a one-click checkout link to confirm and pay on the venue's own page. Works across hundreds of independent restaurants, cafes, bars, and hotels — covering pickup, dine-in, and delivery. Every order receives the venue's allergen and dietary metadata, so you can flag concerns up front. We never auto-charge cards: you always confirm and pay on the venue's checkout page, exactly as if you'd browsed their site directly. Sign in once with your Ordering.Tools account to save name, phone, and email so future orders are one tap away.
