About APiM Galaxy

APiM Galaxy is an MCP (Model Context Protocol) server, built by A Piece of Map Inc., that adds business-development and market-entry research to the AI you already use — ChatGPT, Claude, or Claude Code. You name a company you want to reach or a market you want to enter, and the connector maps who they actually work with, finds the warmest introduction path, and returns a prioritized plan — with every relationship checked against its source, and unverified ties flagged. The full research is free, with no usage limit. With an existing APiM Galaxy Pro account signed in, the same run also delivers a branded report page with a one-click PDF and a saved company profile. Learn more at galaxy.apieceofmap.com/connector.
