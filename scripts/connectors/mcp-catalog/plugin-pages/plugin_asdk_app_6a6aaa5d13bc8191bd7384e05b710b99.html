About Atlas AI Agent

Atlas AI Agent brings your Atlas workspace into ChatGPT, with three assistants in one place. Ask for a briefing and the executive assistant reads your connected mailbox and calendar to tell you what needs attention today, what decisions are pending, and what you're still waiting on. Ask about sales and you get live pipeline figures, your leads ranked by score across campaigns, and a personalised outreach email drafted for whichever lead you name. Ask about marketing and you get channel performance, everything in the content queue with its status, whatever is sitting in your approvals, and new post drafts on request. Nothing is sent or published from ChatGPT. Outreach emails and social posts come back as drafts you approve inside Atlas, so the assistant can never speak to a customer on your behalf without you seeing it first. Connecting is a normal sign-in, and permissions are granted per assistant: you can give ChatGPT your marketing without handing over your inbox, and disconnect any time from Atlas under Connected apps.
