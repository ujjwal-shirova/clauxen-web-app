About TraderSpy

TraderSpy connects ChatGPT to live crypto futures data, so you can ask about the market instead of tab-hopping between exchanges. Ask what the AI signal engine has published for a coin and you get the entry, the take-profit ladder, the stop and what actually happened to earlier signals — not just a direction. Ask what the whales are doing and you get real positions held by top-performing traders on Binance, Hyperliquid, Bybit and OKX, plus a leaderboard scored on PnL, win rate, ROI and consistency. Prices, candles and 13 technical indicators are one question away, and with your own account connected you can ask how far a position sits from liquidation. Every tool is read-only. TraderSpy cannot place, close or modify an order and has no withdrawal or transfer tool — it answers questions, you place your own trades. A free account is enough to start.
