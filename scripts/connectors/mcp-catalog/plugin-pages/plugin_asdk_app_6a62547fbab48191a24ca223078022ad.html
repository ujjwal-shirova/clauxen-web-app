About Clear Energy Facts

Clear Energy Facts gives you an up-to-date list of the best true fixed-rate electricity plans, along with the top free-nights and free-weekends plans on the market. Instead of wading through confusing offers and hidden fees, you get straightforward plan data you can trust. When paired with your Smart Meter Texas (SMT) usage data, it helps match your actual consumption patterns to the plan that will genuinely cost you the least — whether that's a steady fixed rate or a plan built around free nights and weekends.
