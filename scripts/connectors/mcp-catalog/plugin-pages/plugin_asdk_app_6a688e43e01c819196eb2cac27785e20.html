About Influencer Hero

Influencer Hero is an influencer marketing platform for consumer brands. This connector lets you run your influencer program in conversation instead of clicking through the dashboard. Discovery. Search a database of creators across Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube by audience demographics, engagement, and location. Pull full audience reports and verified contact details. Outreach. List and inspect campaigns, add creators to them, and review the email flows and scheduled sends attached to each one. CRM. Query your deal pipeline by campaign, board, funnel stage, country, or follower count. Open a deal for its history, notes, tasks, and scheduled communications. Create deals, move them between funnel stages, and log notes and tasks. Collaborations and performance. Break down a collaboration's orders, revenue, top products, and performance over time. Pull campaign reports across date ranges, and review payouts and payout analytics. Content. Search captured UGC across creators, campaigns, brands, and products, and manage creator submissions through review and approval. Access follows the signed-in user. The connector inherits that user's role and brand permissions exactly as the web app does, so it can never read or change anything the user could not reach directly. Discovery, audience reports, and contact lookups consume account credits and say so before running.
