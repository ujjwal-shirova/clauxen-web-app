About Postbeam

Postbeam connects ChatGPT to your LinkedIn content workflow so you can draft, review, schedule, and analyze posts without leaving the chat. Once connected with your Postbeam account, ChatGPT works directly with your real posts, your writing guidance, and your performance data. Turning a chat box into the place you actually run your LinkedIn presence & strategy. Draft in your voice, from your real work: Ask ChatGPT to write a post and it pulls in your Postbeam content training, your mission, audience, format rules, and content sources so every draft sounds like you, not generic. Because it's working inside ChatGPT, it can also draw raw material from the other tools you've connected (Slack threads, Gmail, meeting recorders like Fellow or Granola) and turn a real customer calls, shipped features, or team wins into finished posts. Drafts are saved straight into Postbeam for review. Edit, organize, and manage your queue: Review your most recent drafts, pull up any single post, and edit its copy or title — all by chatting. You can attach media: images, video, or carousel posts. List posts by status (draft, approved, scheduled) to see exactly where everything stands. Approve and schedule when you're ready: When a draft is ready, ChatGPT can approve it and schedule it to publish to your connected LinkedIn at a specific time. Nothing publishes automatically drafts stay drafts until a person decides otherwise, and approving or scheduling respects the permissions on your account. Understand what's working: Ask what's performing and get answers from your cached analytics: account-level totals (impressions, reactions, comments, reshares, followers gained, members reached) with a daily trend, plus per-post breakdowns over 30-, 90-, or 365-day windows. Use it to spot your best formats and hooks, then immediately draft your next post in that proven style. Built for teams and agencies: Postbeam supports multiple accounts in one place. ChatGPT can list every account you can access, so agencies and teams can switch between clients or teammates and keep each person's drafts, approvals, and analytics cleanly separated.
