About Werify

Werify lets users search their own WhatsApp archive — conversations they imported from a chat export or captured from a number they connected. Through ChatGPT they can find a conversation, read its transcript a page at a time (voice notes included, as text), and search inside it. Read-only: it cannot send messages or change anything.
