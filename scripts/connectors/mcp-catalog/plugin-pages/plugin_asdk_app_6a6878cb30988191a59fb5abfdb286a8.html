About Sydium

Sydium runs your social media in your own voice. Connect your Sydium account and ChatGPT can draft captions the way you actually write (learned from what you have already published), schedule or publish to up to ten platforms (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, X, Facebook, Pinterest, Bluesky, Threads, Mastodon), check how posts performed, pull today's content idea, and manage the upcoming queue - edit, reschedule, or cancel anything still scheduled. Agencies can act for individual clients with the same team-scoped permissions as the Sydium app. Publishing actions are explicit: ChatGPT confirms before anything lands on a public feed. Requires a Sydium account.
