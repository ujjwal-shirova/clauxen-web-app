About Prezi

Prezi's plugin takes slides you already have, made in ChatGPT, generated in another tool, or uploaded as a PowerPoint or PDF, and places them on Prezi's open, zoomable canvas, turning a flat deck into a highly visual, interactive presentation. You get a full suite of presentation, engagement, and sharing tools that go beyond static slides: engagement features like polls, quizzes, and Q&A help you teach, sell, and give your ideas traction long after the slides are made. Already have a deck? Bring it in. Or describe a topic and Prezi will build one for you, with an inline animated preview as it renders. Revise in place: add, edit, reorder, or remove slides and switch themes. Present and share your way: Present live with your content next to you in an interactive video with Newscaster mode Share and export it as a narrated video or a podcast Share one interactive link where your audience can answer polls and quizzes, submit and upvote questions, and respond to word clouds live or on their own time Export to PDF, PowerPoint, or SCORM Every deck lands in your own Prezi account. New decks are unlisted: they are not listed publicly or indexed, and anyone with the share link can open them. You can change a deck's visibility at any time in Prezi. Signing in with your Prezi account is required. Sample prompts: @Prezi create an interactive lesson about [topic] for [audience]. Include an opening poll, a visual explanation, a demonstration, two knowledge checks, a word cloud, and a final quiz. @Prezi turn the attached presentation into a zoomable, interactive presentation for [audience]. Create a clear presentation path and add an opening poll, a word cloud after [section], a moderated Q&A, a five-question quiz, and a feedback activity. @Prezi review this presentation and add audience activities in the most useful places. Include an opening poll, a midpoint word cloud, a moderated Q&A, a five-question closing quiz, and a feedback form.
