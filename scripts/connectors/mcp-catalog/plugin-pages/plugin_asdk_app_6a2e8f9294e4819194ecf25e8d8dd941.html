About AuthorityPrompt

AuthorityPrompt helps organizations create, manage, and improve the trusted information AI systems use to understand their business. As AI search and AI assistants increasingly influence customer decisions, companies need more than websites and marketing content. They need a trusted, structured, and verifiable company record that AI systems can reliably interpret. AuthorityPrompt provides a complete AI Visibility and AI Resolution platform that helps organizations understand, improve, and manage how they are represented across AI ecosystems. With AuthorityPrompt, you can: • Run LLM Visibility Assessments to evaluate how AI systems understand your company • Identify gaps, inconsistencies, and missing information affecting AI understanding • Receive prioritized Growth Tasks with actionable recommendations • Monitor AI Description Changes over time • Build and maintain an AIO Knowledge Profile for your organization • Create a Canonical AI Profile that serves as an authoritative source of truth • Manage Verified Claims supported by trusted evidence and sources • Organize company facts through a centralized Fact Center • Analyze trust, credibility, and source consistency with AI Trust Intelligence • Discover and manage Trusted Sources that influence AI-generated answers • Generate AI Action Pages designed for machine-readable consumption • Create structured data, JSON-LD, llms.txt, and other AI-ready formats • Review LLM Analytics and visibility reports across AI systems • Configure Setup Agents to guide implementation and optimization • Connect Cloudflare and Google Analytics for visibility and performance insights • Manage multiple companies, brands, and client profiles from a single workspace AuthorityPrompt is designed for businesses, agencies, consultants, SaaS companies, startups, and enterprise teams that want to improve AI understanding, strengthen trusted company information, and maintain an authoritative presence across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Apple Intelligence, and other AI systems. Whether you are building your first AI-ready company profile or managing AI visibility at scale, AuthorityPrompt provides the infrastructure needed to create trusted company information AI systems can rely on.
