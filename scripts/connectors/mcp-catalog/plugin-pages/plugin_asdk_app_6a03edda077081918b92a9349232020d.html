About AB Projects

AB (ActionBridge) provides a secure and reliable integration layer between your AI agents and Microsoft Teams. It enables your models to interact directly with your workspace, transforming passive AI insights into active participants in your team's daily operations. Key Features Direct Teams Connectivity: Seamlessly link your AI models to Microsoft Teams channels and private chats. Workflow Automation: Trigger real-time messages, notifications, and status updates based on agent-driven logic. Secure Integration: Designed as a professional conduit to ensure stable data flow between LLMs and enterprise communication hubs. Why You’ll Love It AB eliminates the need to manually relay information between your AI tools and your team. By allowing your agent to communicate where the work happens, it reduces context switching and ensures that AI-generated actions are delivered instantly to the right stakeholders. Whether you are automating project updates or facilitating AI-driven collaboration, AB makes your agents more actionable and effective.
