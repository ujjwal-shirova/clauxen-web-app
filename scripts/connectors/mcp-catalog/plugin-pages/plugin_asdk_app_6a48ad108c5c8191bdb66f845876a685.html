About Nudges

Just tell ChatGPT what to remember and when. Nudges confirms the exact time, then emails you the moment it's due. No app to install, no phone number, no cap on how many you set, and no paywall. It understands the way you actually talk: "in 30 minutes," "tomorrow at 9," "every morning at 8," "every other Friday." Every reminder comes back with the exact local time and the inbox it's headed to, so you catch a mistake before it matters. Ask for your reminders any time, and cancel any one in a sentence. Nudges is free and unlimited, and it reaches you in the inbox you already check. Your email is verified the moment you connect, so there's nothing to set up. We deliver reminders to that address and count basic usage events to run the service. We never store what your reminders say.
