About Kuliko AI

Bring your Kuliko study library into the conversation. Connect Kuliko to turn your own documents and notes into active study material — flashcards, summaries, practice quizzes, and mind maps — and review it all without leaving the chat. Kuliko's methods are built on well-established learning science: spaced repetition and retrieval practice, two of the most studied techniques for moving knowledge into long-term memory. Ask to generate flashcards from a subject, pull up the summary of a document you uploaded, or run through a quiz and get your results. With Kuliko Pro, you can review flashcards on a spaced-repetition schedule that surfaces each card right when you're about to forget it, take full quizzes, and explore concepts visually with mind maps. Everything stays tied to your Kuliko account, so the subjects, documents, and progress you build here show up in your dashboard too. Whether you're prepping for an exam or just trying to retain what you read, Kuliko keeps your studying grounded in how memory actually works.
