About Whiteboard by Athena AI

Whiteboard by Athena AI (Athenaboard) is a AI-native Collaborative Whiteboard that can generate and share AI contents in realtime with your team. How to Get Started - Simply describe what you want (e.g., "wireframes for a mobile app", "create a flowchart for user login", "show me the quadratic formula", "generate an image of a sunset") - The app will automatically use the appropriate tool to render your request - Use "add to whiteboard" to save any visualization for later Core Features 1. Diagram Visualization - Render complex diagrams directly in ChatGPT including: flowcharts, sequence diagrams, class diagrams, state diagrams, ER diagrams, Gantt charts, pie charts, mindmaps, timelines, and more - Powered by Mermaid.js for beautiful, interactive diagrams - Diagrams can be copied or added to your whiteboard 2. Wireframe Recommendation - Brainstorm UI/UX designs with your team in realtime - Over 10,000 editable templates for the AI to pick from - Get design components like buttons, cards, navigation elements, forms, etc. - Supports mobile, web, desktop, and tablet platforms 3. Collaborative Whiteboard - Add any visualization (diagrams, wireframes, images, LaTeX formulas) to a realtime collaborative whiteboard - Collaborate with your team in real-time - Persistent storage - your whiteboards are saved and accessible anytime - Each user has an active board that persists across conversations 4. LaTeX Visualization - Render complex scientific formulas and math equations in ChatGPT - Supports inline equations, full documents, and chemistry notation - Beautiful typesetting using KaTeX and full LaTeX compilation 5. Generate Image and Print on Product - Generate AI images from text descriptions with various styles (realistic, artistic, cartoon, sketch, abstract) - Create and buy custom products: T-shirts, mugs, hoodies, and pillows - Products are created via Printify and can be purchased directly
