About VisualSong

VisualSong turns a creative prompt into a real-time audio-reactive visual inside ChatGPT. Choose a local audio file in the player and watch the visual respond to its spectrum and history. Audio stays on your device and is never uploaded to VisualSong. No separate VisualSong account, payment, or persistent storage is required.
