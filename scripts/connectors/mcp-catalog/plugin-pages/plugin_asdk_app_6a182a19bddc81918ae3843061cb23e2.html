About Validis (US)

Validis connects ChatGPT to transaction level accounting data extracted from a company's source ledgers. Search and select engagements, pull standardized general ledger, accounts receivable, accounts payable, and trial balance data through a single consistent schema, regardless of the underlying accounting system. Run built-in analytics including financial ratios, AR and AP aging, working capital, revenue quality, ledger integrity, and fraud indicators, or query the underlying data directly. Designed for lenders, auditors, and finance teams who need fast, reliable access to financial data inside their AI workflows.
