About Pulse by PassBy

Pulse provides real-world retail intelligence for physical stores across the United States. Powered by PassBy’s proprietary data, it lets users ask simple questions like which stores are the busiest, how many people visited a location, or how performance compares across cities and brands. Answers are delivered instantly, without dashboards or analyst support. Pulse powers analysis and reports with aggregated, privacy-safe retail data to help users understand what’s happening in physical retail.
