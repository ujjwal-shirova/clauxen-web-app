About Hero by Vivun

Meet Hero, your AI teammate for Sales. Hero can join your calls, write your follow-ups, research accounts, and help you keep every deal moving. This connector provides ChatGPT read-only access to your Hero data so you can get Hero’s insights wherever you work best. What you can do in ChatGPT with the Hero connector • Search meeting transcripts by keyword, meaning, date range, or workspace to pull the full transcript with per-speaker segments. • Find and read Hero-generated artifacts like call summaries, account research, saved notes, and uploaded files. • Browse and revisit your Hero conversation threads. • Get a list of your Workspaces (accounts and deals) to scope your work. Example prompts • “Summarize my calls with Acme this month, and draft a follow-up email.” • “Can you tell me what objections came up during yesterday’s demo with Hooli?” • “Pull the account research Hero wrote for Globex and turn it into a one-pager.” • “Find every call where pricing came up this quarter.” Security and Privacy • Sign in with secure OAuth: no passwords or API keys to manage. • Strictly read-only: ChatGPT can retrieve your Hero data but cannot create, modify, or delete anything. • Scoped to your own account: ChatGPT only sees data you already have access to in Hero • Requires a Hero account: get started at meethero.ai.
