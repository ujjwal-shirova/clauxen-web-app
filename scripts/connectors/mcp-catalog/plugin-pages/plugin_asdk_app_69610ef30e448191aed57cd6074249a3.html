About Pick your match

pickyourmatch finds travellers the exact bookable accommodation — not a room category. Every unit is individually described down to the view, the floor, the beds and the per-unit child and dog allowance, with data refreshed nightly from each property's GauVendi system. Ask for "an apartment in Schladming for 2 adults, 2 kids and a dog, 16–19 August" and the app returns units that are actually available for those dates, with the real nightly rate and a direct booking link that opens the property's own booking calendar with the exact unit and dates preselected — no portals, no middleman, no fees on top. Also included: themed promotions and packages (dog holidays, romance, wellness, culinary) with included extras, searchable by city or theme. The app is read-only, needs no account and processes no personal data.
