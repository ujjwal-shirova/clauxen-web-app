About Crunchbase

Crunchbase is the leading provider of private-company prospecting and research data, used by salespeople, entrepreneurs, investors, and market researchers to find and evaluate companies. This connector brings that data into your conversation. Ask in plain language and get structured answers back. Predictions Crunchbase models what a company is likely to do next: raise funding, get acquired, go public, grow, stay private, shut down, or cut headcount, along with an estimated current valuation. Each prediction comes with a confidence tier, so you can sort a market by likelihood rather than reading profiles one at a time. Insights Written summaries of what's happening at a company right now, covering growth trajectory, recent news, R&D activity, personnel changes, investor activity, and market position. Company, funding, and people data Profiles with descriptions, headcount, revenue range, location, and industry. Full funding round history with amounts, dates, types, and investors. Acquisitions, IPOs, funds, and investments. Founders, executives, and leadership changes. Similar-company matching, plus many more signals. Build lists you can return to Save companies as you find them, so a search you run today becomes a pipeline you can monitor over time. Requires a Crunchbase account. Available data depends on your subscription tier.
