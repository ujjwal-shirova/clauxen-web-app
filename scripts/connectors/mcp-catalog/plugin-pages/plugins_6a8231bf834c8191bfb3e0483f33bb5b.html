About CarSleuth

Do not buy the badge—investigate the exact car. CarSleuth turns a listing URL, screenshot, VIN, vehicle-history report, or pasted vehicle details into an evidence-led used-car assessment. It resolves the exact engine and transmission before judging reliability; maps costly powertrain failure modes; places the vehicle on its mileage-specific maintenance timeline; checks public VIN, recall, auction, accident, title, and listing clues; and examines repairability, fuel requirements, driving dynamics, ownership exposure, and local value. The result is built for action: a clear verdict, confidence level, fair-value range, maximum recommended price, immediate and 12/24/36-month cost exposure, model-specific pre-purchase inspection brief, evidence-seeking seller questions, and walk-away triggers. CarSleuth keeps verified facts, recurring patterns, anecdotes, seller claims, and unknowns separate. It never treats a public VIN search as CARFAX, missing records as a clean history, or brand reputation as proof that a specific car is reliable. Research-based decision support only. It does not replace an independent mechanical inspection or official ownership, title, lien, recall, insurance, and registration checks.
