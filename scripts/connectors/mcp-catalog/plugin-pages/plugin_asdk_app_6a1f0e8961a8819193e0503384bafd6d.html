About Spkr - Chat to Podcast Episode

The ChatGPT Spkr app allows subscribers to convert any ChatGPT answer to a podcast episode for later listening. The podcast episodes can be listened to in the Spkr mobile app or any podcast player. Examples: “Create a 10-minute podcast episode explaining what the world might look like in 2035.” “Create a podcast episode explaining how to build better habits using behavioral science.” “Explain black holes like I’m an intelligent non-scientist.” “Tell the story of how the internet was invented and the key people behind it.” Spkr is an app that allows users to convert articles, documents, emails, and ChatGPT responses to personalize podcasts for later listening.
