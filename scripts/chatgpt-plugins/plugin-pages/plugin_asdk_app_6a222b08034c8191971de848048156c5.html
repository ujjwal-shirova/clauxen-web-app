About Unni

Unni brings one of Asia's largest medical procedure platforms into ChatGPT. Ask in your own words and see the same procedure and event information available in the Unni app at a glance in rich cards. What you can search: • Procedure knowledge: expert-written guides on procedures, side effects, recovery, and procedure comparisons. • Community: real user experiences and doctor-answered Q&A from the Unni community. • Events: current pricing and promotions across thousands of clinics, filterable by procedure, location, consultation language, and price. Unni searches from information pools matched to your country and language, so you can see procedure information and events that are actually available to you. Unni provides reference information only and is not a medical recommendation. Always consult a licensed medical professional before making procedure decisions.
