About TrailVerse

TrailVerse brings national parks exploration into ChatGPT. Live data for 470+ U.S. national parks, monuments, and historic sites — plus day-by-day trip planning. Tell Trailie you’re a beginner with kids visiting Zion in July, and it can build a pace-matched itinerary using live NPS alerts and weather, with hike limits and family-friendly pacing when you ask. Always confirm safety-critical details on NPS.gov before you go. Five focused capabilities: • Plan a trip — Constraint-aware day-by-day itineraries (dates, hike difficulty/distance, pace). Revise the plan in the same chat. Best for NPS parks; also useful for broader U.S. trip ideas. • Park details — Live weather, alerts, entrance fees, and top activities for NPS sites. • Compare parks — Side-by-side 2–4 NPS parks with clear highlights. • Search parks — Find NPS sites by state, activity, or name across the full catalog. • Find events — Upcoming ranger programs, guided tours, and park-hosted events. Read tools use the National Park Service API and live weather. Campground and permit inventory are not guaranteed live — confirm on Recreation.gov or NPS before booking. Free to use in ChatGPT. For saved trips, PDF export, and full Trailie history, continue at nationalparksexplorerusa.com/plan-ai.
