About Wordy- Highlight+Annotate Text

Read and write anything with AI highlighting that explains difficult words, ideas, arguments, and writing techniques as you go. Paste any passage into the chat and get a clean, readable version with highlights that open simple explanations beside the text. Instead of jumping between search results, dictionaries, and websites, you stay focused on what you are reading. Tap a highlighted phrase to see why it matters, what it means, or how it fits into the bigger picture. The app is built for curiosity. Read literature, history, science, news, speeches, legal writing, essays, research papers, blog posts, and your own drafts. Each annotation is created for the specific passage, so the explanations match the surrounding context instead of giving a one-size-fits-all definition. Highlights can explain: • Vocabulary and unfamiliar phrases • Literary devices and writing techniques • Themes, symbols, and motifs • Historical and cultural references • Character development and motivation • Arguments, evidence, and reasoning • Tone, mood, and style • Grammar and sentence structure • Rhetorical strategies • Important facts and key ideas The reading experience stays simple. The original text remains front and center while annotations appear only where they are useful. Open one note at a time to avoid clutter, or move through every annotation in order. Whether you want a quick answer or a deeper explanation, the information is always close to the text that inspired it. Students can use the app to prepare for class, review assigned readings, or understand challenging passages before discussions and exams. Teachers can use it to explore different ways of explaining the same text. Lifelong learners can finally tackle books and articles that once felt too difficult. Writers and editors can also benefit. Paste your own work into the app to see explanations of structure, language, and rhetorical choices. Use the annotations to spot places where readers may need more context or where a sentence could be clearer. Every passage is different. A line from Shakespeare needs different notes than a scientific article or a court opinion. The app adapts its explanations to the text instead of forcing every document into the same template. Designed with a clean interface, the app keeps reading comfortable on phones and tablets. Text is easy to scan, annotations are easy to open, and the layout keeps your place while you explore each note. Whether you are studying for school, preparing for work, learning a new subject, or reading for fun, the app helps you spend less time searching for explanations and more time understanding what you read. Paste a passage, explore the highlights, and discover more in every paragraph. You decide how much detail you want. Read straight through without interruptions, or pause on any highlight when something catches your attention. Short notes give fast answers.
