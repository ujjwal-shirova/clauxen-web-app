About Read AI

Read AI is an AI meeting notetaker and meeting assistant for Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams. Record meetings and get AI meeting notes, summaries, transcripts, action items, key questions, and decisions. Search your meeting history in ChatGPT, catch up on meetings you missed, prepare for upcoming calls, find past conversations, and turn meeting insights into follow-ups and next steps. With the Read AI app, you can: • Search and list your recent meetings, filtered by date or topic • Pull full meeting summaries, transcripts, key questions, and action items into any OpenAI conversation • Send a Read AI meeting agent to join a live meeting on your behalf to take notes • Share meeting reports with teammates by email
