About Vize Rehberim

An information and calculation tool for visa applications by citizens of Türkiye (ordinary/burgundy passport). It answers questions like "Do I need a visa for Japan?", "How much does a German visa cost?" and "How many Schengen days do I have left?" — always with its source. What it covers: · Visa status for 172 countries — 55 visa-free, 23 visa on arrival, 30 e-visa, 64 requiring a consulate application · Per country: the fee, the published processing time, the application centre and the required documents · Refusal rates for Schengen applications made from Türkiye, based on European Commission statistics (27 countries) · Appealing a refusal: the competent authority, deadline, language and fee for seven Schengen countries, and the meaning of all 17 refusal grounds on the standard form · Calculators: the Schengen 90/180 day allowance, multiple-entry (cascade) eligibility, and the total cost in Turkish lira at the central bank rate How the data is collected: Every amount and duration is verified against an official consulate, ministry or legislative source, quoted verbatim. A value that cannot be verified is not published — nothing is estimated; the field is left empty and said to be empty. Answers carry their source and last-updated date. Only the consulate of the country concerned can grant a visa. We are not an agency: we do not file applications, book appointments, or guarantee approval.
