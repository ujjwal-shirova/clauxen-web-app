About REIzer Deal Analysis

REIzer underwrites a real-estate deal the way an investor would. Give it a price and a few numbers and it returns a 0–100 score, the metrics that matter for that strategy, stress scenarios showing what breaks the deal, every assumption it filled in for you, and a shareable report link. It covers long-term rental, short-term rental, BRRRR, fix-and-flip, multifamily and new construction. It can compare all six against the same property to find its best use, and solve backwards for the highest price you could pay and still hit your return targets. Results are software estimates, not financial advice.
