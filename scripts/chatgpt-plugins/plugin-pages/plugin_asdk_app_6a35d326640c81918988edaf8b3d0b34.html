About Website Launches

Website Launches lets ChatGPT look up a website’s public launch record, including when the site was first detected, whether the owner has verified the listing, and available launch milestones. It provides public launch and presence context for reviewing new or unfamiliar websites, but it does not determine whether a site is safe or unsafe.
