About Izifete

Izifete helps you organise an event in France - a wedding, a birthday, a christening, a corporate seminar or a party - by finding verified local professionals and giving you realistic planning figures before you commit. Search by trade and by city across 15 event trades: caterer, street food, DJ, photographer, videographer, photo booth, decorator, entertainment, pastry chef, florist, equipment rental, private chef, make-up artist, hairdresser and reception venue. Every result is a real published profile on izifete.fr, shown with its city, its trades and its rating. You can also get planning figures taken from Izifete's published guides and calculators - a typical budget per trade, quantities to order, how far ahead to book each professional - and check whether a French company is officially registered from its SIRET number, using public government data. When you are ready, you can send a quote request to a professional. Izifete recaps the details and asks you to confirm first, and the request is then reviewed by the Izifete team before it reaches the professional. Nothing is sent automatically. Izifete covers France and Monaco only. When no verified professional matches a search yet, the plugin says so plainly instead of suggesting someone in another trade or another city.
