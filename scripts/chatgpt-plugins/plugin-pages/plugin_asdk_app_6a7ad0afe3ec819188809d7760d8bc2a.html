About StarryKit

Bring an AI-powered visual design workflow into ChatGPT and Codex. Create, refine, review, and export polished, editable designs through natural conversation. What you can create: AI presentations and pitch decks, posters and flyers, social media graphics, infographics, diagrams, cards, email designs, web concepts, UI mockups, and visual documents. Create presentations: Turn a topic, outline, brief, or existing content into a polished, editable presentation. Create posters: Design event posters, promotional graphics, flyers, and visual art in any supported format. Create social media content: Produce editable designs for Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook, and other channels. Create infographics and diagrams: Transform complex ideas, processes, and data into clear visual stories. Recreate designs: Provide an image, screenshot, website, document, or design.md file as a visual reference. StarryKit analyzes its composition, typography, colors, hierarchy, and visual language, then recreates the direction with editable elements—without requiring a template. Edit and refine: Rewrite complete pages or request focused changes to text, layout, colors, imagery, hierarchy, and formatting while preserving the rest of the design. Review generated page drafts in StarryKit before accepting them into your document. Export designs: Download your finished work as PowerPoint, PDF, SVG, PNG, JPEG, or HTML, or export an editable presentation to Google Slides. Once installed, describe what you want in plain language or invoke StarryKit directly with @StarryKit. Try prompts like: - @StarryKit, create a 10-slide investor pitch deck for a sustainable urban farming startup. - @StarryKit, design a bold poster for an electronic music festival in Tokyo. - @StarryKit, turn this report into a clear, visual infographic. - @StarryKit, recreate this website screenshot as an editable visual design. - @StarryKit, make the headline larger, highlight the key metric, and preserve everything else. - @StarryKit, export this presentation to PowerPoint and Google Slides.
