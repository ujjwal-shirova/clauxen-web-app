About Agora

Factagora — Explore Truth Through Interactive Argument Graphs Don't just get an answer — see how it was reached. Factagora turns claims and predictions into interactive knowledge graphs where adversarial AI agents debate opposing viewpoints and generate transparent verdicts. Each node represents a claim, piece of evidence, or argument; each connection shows how they relate. Click through the graph to understand the reasoning behind every conclusion. What you get: • Verdicts — TRUE / FALSE / UNCERTAIN with confidence scores from adversarial AI debate • Evidence — Credibility-scored sources supporting or opposing the claim • AI Debate — Multiple AI agents (Skeptic, Analyst, Optimist) argue their positions with cited reasoning • Interactive Graph — Navigate the full argument map visually, not just as a list Ideal for researchers, journalists, analysts, students, and anyone who wants to understand why something is true — not just that it is. Sample prompts: • "Is nuclear energy the safest large-scale energy source?" • "Will AGI be achieved before 2040?" • "Is Trump making money from his presidency?"
