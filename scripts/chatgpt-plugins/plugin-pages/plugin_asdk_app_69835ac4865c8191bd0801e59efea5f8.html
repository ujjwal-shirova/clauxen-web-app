About TravExp

TravExp slashes the time to plan a trip that matches your taste by up to 90%, so you can spend more time enjoying the experience and less time stressing over details. This app elevates your travel planning experience on ChatGPT by directly integrating into the conversation flow. You can instantly turn ChatGPT-generated itineraries into rich, visual trip plans that are editable and collaborative. You can even create a brand-new personalized itinerary right from the chat. TravExp creates beautiful, visual itineraries packed with essential details, including places to visit, reviews, seasonal insights, must-do activities, and more. You can also easily setup a planning workspace by directly opening the itinerary on TravExp where you can leverage supporting features like navigation, local tips, exclusive deals, packing essentials, and AI-powered travel assistance.
