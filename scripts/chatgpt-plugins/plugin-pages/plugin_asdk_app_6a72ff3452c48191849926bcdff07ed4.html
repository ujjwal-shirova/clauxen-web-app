About Bravos AI

Bravos AI lets businesses create AI chatbots trained on their own content (website, documents, product catalogs) and deploy them on their website or WhatsApp. This app connects ChatGPT to your Bravos account so you can manage everything without opening the dashboard: check what your customers asked, audit which sources the bot used for each answer, fix knowledge gaps, update your bot's instructions, system prompt and tone, and track usage against your plan. It comes with Bravos' professional judgment built in: the app knows how Bravos works internally and guides you toward changes that actually improve your bot's answers. Requires a Bravos AI account (bravos-ai.com).
