About Atlas Red

Atlas Red is a workspace for structured mind maps. A map is a tree of topics, and every topic in a map shares the same author-defined fields — plain text, or typed ones like a select with its own options, a number or a date — so a map behaves like a small database you can see. Connect your account and you can create maps, read a single topic's neighbourhood without pulling in the whole map, add and reorganise topics, fill in typed fields, link topics across branches, and import or export maps as Markdown, OPML, YAML and other formats. Every change is saved to your account, and anything you do here can be undone — from this conversation or later in the app. The maps persist and stay yours: the same map opens in the Atlas Red web app on any device, on a visual canvas where you can rearrange it, colour it by one of its fields, attach images and share a read-only link.
