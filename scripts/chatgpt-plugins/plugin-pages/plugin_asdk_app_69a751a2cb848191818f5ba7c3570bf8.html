About Bandsintown

Bandsintown provides real-time concert and tour information so you can easily find live music by artist, location, or date. Use Bandsintown to: Find concerts and live music near you tonight, this weekend, or on any date Check if your favorite artists are touring and where they’re playing Find shows by location, venue, or genre Get tour dates and event details, including links to tickets from official ticket sellers Bandsintown covers concerts and live music worldwide, so you can plan with confidence. Try asking: “What concerts are in [city] this weekend?” “Is [artist name] on tour right now?” “Show me music venues in [city]” “What’s playing at [venue name] tonight?” Whether you’re looking for a last-minute show or planning ahead for a major tour, Bandsintown helps you keep track of upcoming live music.
