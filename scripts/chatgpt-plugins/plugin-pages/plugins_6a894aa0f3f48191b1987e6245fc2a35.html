About Creator Workspace

Creator Workspace helps creators organize ChatGPT and Claude exports, research, project state, and writing preferences into an inspectable second brain. It can retrieve the right context, build and maintain a living wiki, preserve a user’s writing voice, draft and structure social content, plan visual outputs, analyze LinkedIn exports, and carry explicit corrections or project state into future work. The package is skills-only and uses host-native file, Python, web, and connector capabilities only when those capabilities are actually available.
