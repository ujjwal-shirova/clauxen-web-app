About Bitcoin Mining Troubleshooter

Analyze Bitcoin ASIC miner logs, fleet scans, spreadsheets, screenshots, repair histories, and power or network evidence. The plugin separates observed facts from inference, highlights missing or conflicting evidence, identifies shared infrastructure patterns, and recommends one safe read-only diagnostic check. It does not reboot miners, change pools, flash firmware, tune hardware, or scan public networks.
