About MoonPay

Buying crypto used to mean leaving your conversation, opening a separate tab, hunting for the right token, and pasting wallet addresses. MoonPay does it from inside ChatGPT. Tell the app what you want "USDC on Solana", "0.1 ETH", "$100 of Bitcoin" and it generates a ready-to-go checkout link in seconds. Click through to moonpay.com, pay with a card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, or bank transfer, and the crypto lands in your wallet. No new account needed if you already have a MoonPay account. The app fills in the asset, chain, and amount; you confirm the rest at the moonpay.com checkout. Supports Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, USDC, and 100+ other assets across 30+ chains. About this app. Buy-link generation only, no funds move or transactions execute inside ChatGPT. All payment activity, KYC, and regulatory controls happen at moonpay.com under MoonPay's licensed entities. Cryptoasset values can rise and fall significantly and your capital is at risk. Supported countries and assets are governed by your KYC outcome and MoonPay's licensing in your jurisdiction.
