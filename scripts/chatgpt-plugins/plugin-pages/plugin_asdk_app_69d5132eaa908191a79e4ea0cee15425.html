About AnyWhereMap - Navigate+Locate

A fantastic way to use maps with ChatGPT! Ask it to display the map for somewhere, then scroll and zoom around. Discuss the visible area with ChatGPT, drop pins, and ask questions. e.g. - "Open an interactive @AnyWhereMap" - "Show me New York City in an interactive @AnyWhereMap" Open the map, scroll to an area and ask: - "What is in this area?" - "Is this a nice place to visit?"
