About SeldonFrame

SeldonFrame is a marketplace of AI agents for service businesses. Find an agent for the job — an AI receptionist that answers and books, a review requester, a lead qualifier, a booking concierge — and put it to work. Describe your business and SeldonFrame spins up a real workspace in under a minute: a live website, booking page, CRM, and AI chatbot on its own web address, no signup. Then browse the marketplace and add agents to handle the busywork. Builders create and list their own agents too, so the catalog keeps growing. Built for local service businesses — trades, clinics, salons, studios — and the agencies that serve them.
