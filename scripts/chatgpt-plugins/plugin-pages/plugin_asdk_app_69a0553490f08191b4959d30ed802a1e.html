About BE-A

BE-A enables to search for available hotels in your target area for real-time availability and prices. And you book directly with the hotel where you can leverage loyalty rates, discount for registered members and much more. With direct connection to hotel's Availability and Rates systems, and in near future also to their Concierge and Booking systems, this is a very powerful all-round Hotel Booking Assistant.
