About Pantry Persona

Everything your favorite kitchen management app does, and more, inside ChatGPT. Pantry Persona remembers what's in your fridge, what your family can and can't eat, and which brands you prefer. It stores your recipes, plans your meals, builds your shopping lists, and tracks the nutrition behind it all, then keeps everything in sync as you cook and shop through the week **Recipes** — Save a recipe from a website, a cooking video, or a social post, or just create one in conversation. Search by ingredient, cuisine, or what you can make right now with what's on hand, and group your favorites into collections. **Meal plans** — Generate a full week of meals in one ask, or build it day by day. Plans respect your household's food preferences and eating rules automatically, and carry per-meal nutrition so you can see how the week shapes up. **Shopping lists** — Visual, interactive lists organized by store. One tap checks items off. Ask for a shopping list from your meal plan and it skips anything already in your pantry. Send it to Instacart when you're ready to buy. **Cook Mode** — Start cooking a recipe and get step-by-step guidance. When you're done, ingredients are deducted from your pantry and leftovers are tracked with expiration dates. **Nutrition** — See calories and macros on every recipe and meal, set your own daily targets for each person, and check how today or the week is stacking up. Log what you actually ate, even a meal out, and it counts toward your totals. **Your kitchen, personalized** — Household profiles store each family member's food preferences, eating rules, favorite brands, and nutrition targets. Every suggestion, plan, and search filters through them so you don't have to repeat yourself. Invite other family members and everyone works from the same pantry, recipes, and shopping lists in real time. Also tracks pantry expiration dates, predicts when you'll run low on staples, learns the brands and prices you buy, and turns grocery receipts into an up-to-date inventory and a clear picture of your spending.
