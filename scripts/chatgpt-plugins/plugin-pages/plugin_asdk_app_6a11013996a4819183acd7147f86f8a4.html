About Purely Snacks

Purely Snacks helps you find healthier snack options that match your preferences. Search for products by type, ingredient, nutrition goal, or dietary attribute, then refine results with requests like “lower sugar,” “more protein,” “gluten free,” “vegan,” or “no peanuts.” The app returns snack options with useful details such as brand, calories, protein, sugar, ingredients, dietary attributes, product categories, and tradeoffs, helping you compare choices quickly before opening the product page.
