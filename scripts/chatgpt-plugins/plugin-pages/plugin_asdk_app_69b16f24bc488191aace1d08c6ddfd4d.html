About trivago

The trivago MCP server lets users search for hotels and other accommodations by travel dates and location, from countries and cities to specific venues, landmarks, or other points of interest. Using the details provided, it returns tailored hotel options with prices from multiple booking sites, plus photos, key accommodation details, and helpful links to learn more. In simple terms, it gives AI assistants an efficient way to help users find the right stay at the right price. You can use the trivago app for Search hotels by destination and dates: "Find me a hotel in Las Vegas for 2 people from 12 July to 20 July 2026" Find budget accommodation near landmarks: "I need a cheap accommodation near the Eiffel Tower for 1 night the 12th of June 2026" Book hotels near event venues: "Find me a hotel near the Etihad Stadium for 12 July to 14 July 2026" Coordinate travel with conferences: "I need accommodation close to the venue of the next KubeCon happening in Europe in 2026" Plan family vacations: "I am searching for 2 rooms for 2 adults and 2 children aged 6 and 10 in Barcelona for Easter 2026"
