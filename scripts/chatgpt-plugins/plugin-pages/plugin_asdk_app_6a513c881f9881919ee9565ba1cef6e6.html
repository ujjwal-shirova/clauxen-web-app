About Kamai

Kamai is an AI blueprint analysis and construction takeoff platform built for contractors, subcontractors, estimators, and construction teams. Kamai helps users extract quantities, measurements, materials, rooms, fixtures, and scope information directly from construction drawings and project documents. Contractors can use Kamai to estimate jobs faster, prepare bids, calculate material requirements, answer questions about blueprints, and reduce hours of manual takeoff work. Kamai supports interior construction and finish trades such as: * Painting and coatings * Drywall and sheetrock * Insulation * Metal and wood framing * Flooring * Ceilings * Doors, windows, and room measurements Kamai also supports additional construction trades and building systems, including: * Electrical * Plumbing * Fixtures and equipment * General construction quantities Users can upload blueprints and ask questions such as: * How many square feet of flooring are required? * How much wall area needs to be painted? * How many sheets of drywall are needed? * How much insulation and framing material is required? * How many doors, sinks, toilets, outlets, or fixtures are shown? * What materials should be ordered for this project? * What is included in the project scope? * Can you generate a takeoff from these drawings? Kamai converts static construction documents into structured, AI-readable project data that can support estimating, bidding, purchasing, RFIs, cost planning, and project execution. For contractors who need to review drawings, estimate construction work, order materials, or complete more bids in less time, Kamai provides an AI-powered way to understand project documents and turn blueprint data into actionable quantities.
