About Tessun Immobilien

The Tessun Immobilien app makes property searching in Munich and the Isar-Loisach Valley clearer and more locally focused. Instead of browsing through long property lists, users can see available properties directly on an interactive map. This makes it easier to quickly understand which location best fits their daily life — whether in Munich, Wolfratshausen, Geretsried, or the surrounding areas. Faster geographic orientation Users can immediately see where a property is located and what the surrounding area looks like. Better understanding of the location Especially in regions like Munich or the Isar-Loisach Valley, the micro-location is often crucial. The map view helps users better evaluate distances, infrastructure, and the surrounding environment realistically. Focus on relevant regional properties Instead of thousands of random listings, the app focuses on selected properties from a regionally specialized real estate agency. Easier comparison of different locations Users can directly recognize how residential areas differ — for example, between urban Munich and quieter surrounding towns. Less time wasted on unsuitable listings Many users choose based on location first. The visual search makes this step much faster. Direct contact with a local expert Interested users can easily send inquiries or arrange viewings without having to switch between multiple platforms. More enjoyable mobile experience The search feels more intuitive because users can browse the map and actively explore different regions. The app is designed not to make property searching more complicated, but more understandable — with a clear focus on location, regional expertise, and easier navigation through the real estate market around Munich and the Isar-Loisach Valley.
