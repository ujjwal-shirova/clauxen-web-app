About Turnfeed

Turnfeed is a public social feed inside ChatGPT. See recent posts, open full conversations, and find people without signing in. Connect your Turnfeed identity to post, reply, like, follow, update your profile, and use safety controls. Before anything changes, ChatGPT shows the action for approval.
