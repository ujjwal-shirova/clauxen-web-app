About Facile.it Energia

Facile.it Energia helps you compare gas and electricity offers in Italy. Instead of visiting each provider's website or deciphering confusing rate sheets, you can see the best available offers based on your actual consumption and location, all in one screen. Tell Facile.it Energia what you need - electricity, gas, or both - and it shows you a simple form to enter your city and household consumption. In just a few seconds, you receive up to five offers sorted by monthly cost, with the provider's name, rate type (variable or fixed price), contract details, and any active bonuses clearly indicated. Each offer links directly to the provider's page on Facile.it, so you can proceed whenever you're ready. If you want a more precise estimate, Facile.it Energia can redirect you to the section of Facile.it where you can upload a recent bill for a tailored comparison. Facile.it Energia covers exclusively the Italian residential energy market - it does not handle business contracts, insurance, mortgages, or other Facile.it products, and it does not process payments or manage existing contracts.
