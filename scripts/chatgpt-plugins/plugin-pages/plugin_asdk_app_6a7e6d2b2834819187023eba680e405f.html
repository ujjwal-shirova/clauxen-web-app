About Scribble

Scribble sends real handwritten cards. A machine holds an actual pen and writes each card and envelope in ink, then we stamp it and put it in the post anywhere in the United States. Nothing is a printed font. Connect this and you can ask ChatGPT to look someone up in your address book, draft the card, and send it — and it shows you a picture of the card first, written in the handwriting that will actually be used, alongside the printed front. Ask to see the handwriting styles and it writes you a real specimen of each. You can also load a spreadsheet of contacts, send a personalised batch where every card says a different name, and set up a rule that sends birthday cards every year on its own. Cards are paid for with credits bought in advance on scribblecards.com, so nothing here takes a payment or links to a checkout. Sending is marked as an action that cannot be undone, so ChatGPT asks before it posts anything. A batch is prepared first and shows real examples of the merged cards before you confirm it. A recurring rule is always created switched off, and reports how many people it can reach, so a person decides before it ever fires. Requires a Scribble business account with credits.
