About GoLinks

GoLinks helps teams find, access, and share workplace knowledge faster by turning long URLs into secure, human-readable go links. With the GoLinks app for ChatGPT, employees can search their company’s GoLinks library, find important resources, and get details about go links without leaving ChatGPT. Ask ChatGPT to look up a go link, search by keyword, summarize what a link is for, review ownership and usage details, or create a new go link for quick access to a resource. GoLinks makes it easier for teams to navigate the apps, documents, dashboards, and information they use every day. Instead of remembering long URLs or searching through multiple tools, employees can simply ask ChatGPT for the go link they need. Go links created by your organization are only accessible based on your workspace permissions, helping keep company resources secure while making knowledge easier to discover. Use GoLinks in ChatGPT to reduce context switching, improve productivity, and give your team a faster way to access trusted workplace resources.
