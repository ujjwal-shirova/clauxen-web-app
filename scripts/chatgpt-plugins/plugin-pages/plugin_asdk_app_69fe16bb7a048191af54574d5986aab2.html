About BrightSite

BrightSite is a website hosting and content management platform. Connect your BrightSite account to build and edit pages, layouts, and reusable components; write, publish, and organize blog posts; manage forms and review submissions; upload and organize media; configure SEO, structured data, redirects, and analytics settings; review site traffic; and manage team members and roles. Content edits are saved to a staged draft first, so you can review changes and then publish them to the live site when they are ready. A staging site and full version history let you preview larger changes and roll back if needed.
