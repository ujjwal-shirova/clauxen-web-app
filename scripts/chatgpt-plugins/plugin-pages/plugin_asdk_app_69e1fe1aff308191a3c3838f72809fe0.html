About Fintech Explainer

Fintech Explainer helps users understand fintech and AI news shaping the financial industry in Europe and the broader fintech ecosystem. The app explains industry developments, market shifts, and emerging technologies in clear language, with context on what they mean for banks, startups, regulators, and financial professionals. Built on the editorial archive of Fintech.hu, a Hungarian fintech publication, the app interprets and explains articles originally published in Hungarian, turning them into structured, easy-to-understand insights.
