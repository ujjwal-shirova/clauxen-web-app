About NiceBabyName — Nameology

NiceBabyName reads and compares baby names with Holistic Nameology — the "Four Aspects" method from the book The Four Aspects. Give it one name and it returns that name's Governing Planet, Temperament, Rising Sign, and Rising Degree, with meanings, a personality reading, and newborn-favorability notes. Give it two to five names and it compares them side by side and flags which are most favorable for a newborn, with each name's strengths and pitfalls. It can also search a database of 160,000+ names by starting letters, gender, and cultural origin to gather candidates. English names are read from their pronunciation, which the app asks the user to confirm; Persian and Arabic names are read exactly from their own letters. The parents' names can be included to refine the reading. Works in English, Persian, and Arabic. For guidance and reflection only.
