About AI Attorney

AI Attorney lets ChatGPT search and cite Pakistani law. It connects to a retrieval system over a corpus of Pakistani judgments and statutory provisions, so answers are grounded in real, cited sources instead of recalled from memory. Use it to: - Find judgments relevant to a legal question, filtered by court year, judges names, exact citation, and courts. - Open a specific case by its citation or ID — with the full summary, holding/ratio, and its citation graph (what it relies on, and which later cases cite it). - Look up statutory provisions (e.g., a section of a code or act) by query. Every case result includes its citation and a link to the source of Ai Attorney library. The connector performs retrieval only; ChatGPT composes the final answer from the returned, cited material.
