About Blossom

A language-learning diary app that helps you study a foreign language by writing your daily journal in it — Blossom. Write in 50+ languages including English, Japanese, Chinese, Korean and more, and improve your foreign-language skills every day. Write as much as you can in the foreign-languages. The AI assistant provides real-time corrections with perfect grammar. Feeling hesitant to start writing in a foreign language? It’s perfectly fine to mix in your native language. The AI engine automatically translates it into natural, context-appropriate foreign-language sentences. Even rough drafts are polished smoothly, the “Blossom Editor” helps you write in any language with ease. Hundreds of thousands of foreign-language journal entries are updated daily on Blossom. Blossom is the world’s first and largest AI language-learning platform that integrates journaling with AI, allowing daily diary writing to naturally turn into language learning. With just one sentence a day, you can build a habit that covers all core language skills — writing, speaking, listening, and reading. With a single global journal written together with AI each day, you get vocabulary study, shadowing, AI tutor conversations, and more. Every language learning feature made possible through journaling.
