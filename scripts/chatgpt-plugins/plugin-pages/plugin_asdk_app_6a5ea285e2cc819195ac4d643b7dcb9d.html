About Expertise Live Chatbot

Expertise AI turns any website into a working AI agent, right inside ChatGPT. Share a URL and it reads the site's key pages, then trains a live agent that answers questions using that site's actual content. The whole thing takes about a minute. You get a link to a live preview where you can start chatting with the agent right away. No account, no setup, nothing to install. Things you can do: • Try it on your own site and see what an AI agent trained on your content would tell your customers. • Test how an agent handles your docs, pricing, and product questions before putting one on your website. Ask ChatGPT to "build an AI agent for mycompany.com" and see for yourself.
