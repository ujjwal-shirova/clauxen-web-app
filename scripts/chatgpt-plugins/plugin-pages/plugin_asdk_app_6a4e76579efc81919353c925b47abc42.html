About WunderCineMate

WunderCineMate helps you to discover new movies and rate/manage your favorites! You can use WunderCinaMate in two modes * without sign-in: you can browse and search for public data, like trending movies or actors of a particular feature * with signin: with a TMDB account you can manage your watchlists and rate movies WunderCineMate is powered by The Movie DB (TMBD) - you need to create an account here first to be able to sign-in via WunderCineMate: https://www.themoviedb.org/
