About Yaps Audio Cleaner

Clean noisy speech recordings without uploading them. Yaps desktop removes background noise, hiss, static, room noise, and other distractions while preserving the voice. Recommended is the best general-purpose result, Quick gives long files a fast first pass, and Maximum accuracy performs the most thorough restoration when quality matters more than time. Compare the original and cleaned versions in Yaps, keep multiple attempts, and move from a Quick result to a deeper clean in one click. Requires a local-capable ChatGPT Desktop Work or Codex session; ChatGPT web and cloud sessions cannot reach the installed Yaps engine. Users can also run the feature directly in Yaps.
