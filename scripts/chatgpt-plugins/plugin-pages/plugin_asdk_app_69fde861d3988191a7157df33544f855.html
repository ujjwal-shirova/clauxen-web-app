About Ask Tarot Cards

Tarot Reading is an interactive tarot app for personal insight, reflection, and intuitive exploration. Ask a question, choose your preferred tarot spread, pull the cards yourself, and receive a personalized reading based on your question and the cards you draw. The app supports a one-card pull, two-card spread, three-card spread, and the deeper seven-card Horseshoe spread. Whether you are looking for a quick daily card, clarity around a specific situation, or a more detailed spread reading, Tarot Reading helps you explore the messages, themes, and symbolism of the cards in relation to your question. Each reading is tailored to your chosen spread and card selection, offering a thoughtful interpretation that can help you reflect on your emotions, choices, relationships, and current circumstances through the lens of tarot.
