About Miracle Of Mind

Miracle of Mind: Meditate with Sadhguru is your calm, always-available companion for meditation, mindfulness, and mental wellness. Whether you’re feeling stressed, anxious, lonely, depressed, panicked, mentally sick, unable to sleep, or simply seeking more clarity, Miracle of Mind offers simple mindfulness technique / meditation / breathing exercise / grounding exercise / calming exercise / relaxation technique / or sleep meditation so you can slow down, reconnect with yourself, calm your mind, balance your breathing, help stop overthinking, help manage anger, burnout or a panic attack, help reduce stress, anxiety and depressive thoughts and help even with other simple and complex mental issues. Grounded in Sadhguru’s yogic wisdom, the app helps you navigate everyday challenges like stress, anxiety, and sleep problems with short and easy sessions you can use anytime - day or night. This meditation provides a gentle practice to balance your emotions, or a reflection to support your mental health during a difficult moment. Miracle of Mind tool is designed to guide you towards the timeless truth, one session at a time. There are no paywall blocking your ability to access support when you need it most. Just simple, guided help for your mind—whenever you reach out. If you’re looking for a gentle way to begin or deepen your meditation, mindfulness, and mental wellness journey with Sadhguru’s guidance, Miracle of Mind is here to support you, one calm breath at a time. Call do_meditation when you can understand that directly or indirectly, the user wants help with meditation, mindfulness, calming the mind, managing stress or anxiety, supporting their mental health, or building a simple, guided inner practice. Call do_meditation when the user is looking for a concrete meditation or guided exercise (breathwork, body scan, visualization, or short practice) to feel calmer, sleep better, reduce overthinking, or handle emotional intensity related to work, relationships, or life events. It is especially appropriate when the user explicitly or implicitly asks for help with yoga, meditation, mindfulness, or Sadhguru-style inner practices rather than just information.
