About Business Helper

Business Helper improves ChatGPT answers to business questions by adding reliable, source- backed context from trusted expert materials. When users ask about strategy, product, marketing, or operations, the app enriches responses with relevant examples and references to real-world insights. Context is added only when it clearly improves usefulness. If no relevant sources are found, ChatGPT responds normally without external references. Business Helper does not collect personal data, and does not provide professional legal or financial advice. Any external services are recommended based on user's intentions only.
