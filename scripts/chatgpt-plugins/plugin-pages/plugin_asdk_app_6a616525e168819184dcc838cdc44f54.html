About Trivana Study — Quiz My Notes

Trivana Study turns what you're learning into a playable, AI-hosted practice quiz. Paste up to 2000 characters of notes, share a supported public webpage URL, name a subject, or ask to practice material from the current conversation. Trivana builds questions from that material and returns an unlisted link you can play or share with a study group. An unlisted quiz is not automatically featured publicly, but anyone with its link can open and play it. Choose a quick 5-question check, a balanced 7-question round, or a 10-question deep dive in 10 languages with one of 8 AI host personalities. Questions remain on screen while you answer, scoring is automatic, and your selected host guides the round; voice availability varies by host, language, and production level. Players do not need an account. When available, use Save & track scores on the result card to keep plays, scores, and replays. Useful for class revision, lecture review, certification and exam practice, self-testing, and checking whether the material actually stuck. Trivana Study does not ingest ChatGPT file attachments or PDFs; paste the relevant text or provide a supported public webpage URL instead.
