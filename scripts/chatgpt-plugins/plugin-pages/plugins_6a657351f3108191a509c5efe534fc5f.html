About Cashback Card

Name a store and get the card in your wallet that nets the most on that purchase. Accounts for how the merchant actually codes (Target is a discount store, not a supermarket), spending caps you have partly used, categories you have not activated, what your points are really worth, and foreign transaction or fuel surcharge fees. Verifies rates against issuer terms rather than quoting them from memory. Covers the US, India and the UK.
