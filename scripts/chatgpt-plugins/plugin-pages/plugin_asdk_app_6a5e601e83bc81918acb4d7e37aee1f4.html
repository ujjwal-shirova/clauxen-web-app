About AI Leads Scout

AI Lead Scout helps businesses find qualified B2B prospects through guided AI research. Before it begins researching, AI Lead Scout interviews you to understand your business, services, target market, preferred locations, qualification requirements, and ideal customer profile. It then uses that information to identify relevant business prospects, explain why each company may be a good fit, check for duplicates, and save approved leads to your AI Lead Scout dashboard for review and follow-up. AI Lead Scout is designed for business owners, marketers, agencies, and sales teams that want a more focused and organized way to find B2B prospects that match their specific goals. A paid ChatGPT subscription and an active AI Lead Scout account are required. Visit AILeadScout.net to learn more and create your account before using the plugin.
