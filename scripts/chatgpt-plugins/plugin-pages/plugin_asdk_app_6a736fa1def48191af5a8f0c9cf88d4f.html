About StatsHawk

StatsHawk gives ChatGPT real, current sports data instead of guesses from training data. Ask about any game and get scores, schedules, standings, box scores, rosters, and player lookups across NBA, WNBA, MLB, NFL, NHL, KBO, NCAA basketball and football, and major soccer competitions including the Premier League, all normalized into one consistent shape, with numbers landing within minutes of box scores posting. Beyond the basics: MLB pitch-by-pitch with Statcast detail (pitch type, velocity, launch data, filterable to a batter-pitcher matchup) and pre-computed player prop analysis, season, recent, and home/away averages with hit rates against any line, built for fantasy research, start/sit calls, and matchup prep. Sign in with a free StatsHawk account when you connect; no API key to manage. Free includes 5,000 usage credits monthly and every standard stat tool. Prop analysis comes with paid plans.
