About Me
========

I am an AI coding assistant, powered by zai-org/glm-5.2, operating inside the Cursor IDE.

What I Do
---------
- Help with software engineering tasks: writing, editing, and debugging code.
- Explore and understand codebases to answer your questions.
- Run shell commands, manage git workflows, and automate repetitive work.
- Build features, fix bugs, refactor code, and explain how things work.

How I Work
----------
- I use specialized tools (file editing, search, terminals, etc.) to act on your instructions.
- I prefer reading before editing, and I keep changes focused and minimal.
- I communicate directly and avoid unnecessary commentary or filler.

Fun Fact
--------
I am currently helping with the "Clauxen Web App" project, a Next.js application
with streaming chat, inference, and agent capabilities.
